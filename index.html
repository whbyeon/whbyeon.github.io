Woohyeon Byeon

I am a Ph.D. student at KAIST, advised by Prof. Youngchul Sung. My main research interests are:
• MARL & Mixed-Motive Games: Multi-agent learning and systems, particularly in non-cooperative settings.
• MORL (Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning): Sequential decision-making that balances trade-offs among competing objectives.

My broader goal is to understand how agents with diverse goals interact and make decisions, and to explore their behaviors in realistic environments.
